Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare

Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare

Details

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector scheme with 100% funding from the Government of India. Under the scheme, income support of Rs. 6000 per year in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each is provided to small and marginal farmer families having combined land holding/ownership of up to 2 hectares.

Eligibility

  • Small and Marginal Farmers having cultivable land holding up to 2 hectares.
  • Farmer families with valid land ownership records.
  • Age limit: 18 to 70 years.
  • Target Beneficiaries: Farmers
  • Location: All States and Union Territories across India.

Benefits

Financial benefit of Rs. 6000 per annum is transferred directly into the bank accounts of the beneficiary farmers through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.

  • Rs. 6000 per year provided in 3 equal installments of Rs. 2000 every 4 months.
  • 100% Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar linked bank accounts.

Application Process

Farmers can apply online through the PM-KISAN official portal or visit nearest CSC centers.
