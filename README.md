🌾 RuralEdge

AI-Powered Rural Business Feasibility & Loan Intelligence Platform

RuralEdge helps rural entrepreneurs evaluate business opportunities, understand local market conditions, estimate project costs, and identify suitable financing options.

It combines market intelligence, financial analysis, government scheme information, and AI assistance into one platform.

🚀 Key Features

* 📍 Local Market Analysis — Analyze nearby businesses, competition, and market reach.
* 📊 Business Feasibility — Opportunity, SWOT, competitor, and risk analysis.
* 💰 Project Costing — Estimate setup costs, operating expenses, and working capital.
* 🏦 Loan Intelligence — Calculate loan requirements and identify applicable financing schemes.
* 🧮 Financial Analysis — EMI, interest, repayment schedule, profitability, and break-even analysis.
* 🤖 AI Assistant — Get simple, personalized explanations and business insights.
* 🌐 Multilingual Support — Designed for accessibility across rural communities.
